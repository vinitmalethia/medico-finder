General Stay & Accommodation Status for Abroad Students

Everything you need to know about housing and food while studying medicine abroad.

Mandatory 1st Year Hostels

Most universities mandate 1st-year international students to stay in on-campus university hostels for safety, warden supervision, and acclimatization.

Universal Indian Mess

Indian food cooked by Indian chefs is available universally in top colleges in Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and Kyrgyzstan (~₹10,000 – ₹12,000/month).

Private Flat Rentals

From the 2nd/3rd year, students can rent private apartments with classmates, lowering monthly living expenses to around ₹12,000 – ₹15,000.
